Rule

  1. Respect everyone’s opinions, even if you disagree with them.
  2. No hate speech, harassment, or personal attacks of any kind.
  3. Do not post content that promotes violence or illegal activities.
  4. Avoid sharing false information or misleading content.
  5. Keep posts appropriate and free from explicit or offensive material.
  6. Do not share personal or private information about yourself or others.
  7. Use respectful language and avoid unnecessary profanity.
  8. Spamming, flooding, or repetitive posting is not allowed.
  9. Report posts that violate the rules instead of engaging in conflict.
  10. Remember that behind every post is a real person—be kind.
